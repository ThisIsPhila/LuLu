## Review corrections and limits

Review found and fixed false old-snapshot promotion after invalid input, record-stage failures blocking new draft edits, misleading retry actions, finalization acknowledgement loss and blocked-open connection leakage. Focused regression cases cover each. No known blocking implementation defect remains in this bounded slice.

Durability means **acknowledged local storage snapshots**, not guaranteed last-keystroke recovery after process/power loss or browser eviction. Quick exit never waits for persistence; controller stop initiates no new vault operation or raw flush. A transaction already started can finish an encrypted commit around a lock and have its acknowledgement suppressed; subsequent recovery/reconciliation checks stored truth. No plaintext is persisted, and late reads remain authorization-checked. JavaScript cleanup is not secure memory erasure.

The browser-held key remains independent of the passkey and vulnerable to same-origin/device compromise; browser-data loss can destroy access. Full rollback/deletion cannot be detected. No offline app shell/unlock, cross-device key transfer/recovery, media, autosave while locked, backup/export/deletion/retention, contextual detector/model transport, companion delivery, Trails or support integration is claimed. Conflicting targeted drafts remain preserved; automatic merge/rebase is not implemented. Device times/hashes do not establish legal validity or incident truth.

Native owner acceptance remains open: real passkey entry → type/autosave → background/restart → private reopen → recover/promote → inspect original/history, with target-phone keyboard, actual quota/eviction and interrupted-save testing. Next broader work remains tracked in `docs/product/2026-10-25-task-status.md`; stop after this bounded local deliverable.
