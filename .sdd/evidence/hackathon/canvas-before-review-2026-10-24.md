## Section 1

Gender-based violence, harassment and abuse remain significant challenges in South Africa. The HSRC's 2024 National Gender-Based Violence Study found that approximately 35.5% of women aged 18 and older had experienced physical and/or sexual violence during their lifetime.




Beyond the violence itself, individuals face barriers to safely documenting incidents, preserving evidence, accessing appropriate support and deciding whether to disclose or report their experiences. Existing processes can require individuals to expose sensitive information before they are ready, while records may be fragmented across messages, photographs and personal accounts. There is a need for a discreet, accessible and survivor-directed way to preserve experiences, understand available options and seek assistance without surrendering control over personal information.

## Section 2

LuLu is a discreet, mobile-first personal safety and documentation platform embedded within a fully functional everyday journal or storybook application.




Through an intentionally concealed, passkey-protected experience, users can privately document incidents, preserve photographs, messages, audio and documents, and build an organised chronology of events. Intelligent contextual transformation converts identifying details and narrative perspective into coherent alternative accounts while maintaining protected access to their original meaning.




Additional capabilities include evidence-integrity records, user-controlled selective disclosure, discreet exit, cross-device recovery, relevant support resources and referral pathways, and exploration of privacy-preserving insights into recurring patterns. Users remain in control of whether, when and how they seek assistance or share information.

## Section 3

Your experience.  Your evidence. Your decision.




LuLu enables people to safely document sensitive experiences, preserve important information and explore support options without being pressured into disclosure or formal reporting.




By combining an ordinary everyday application with discreet access, intelligent narrative concealment, evidence preservation and selective sharing, LuLu gives individuals continuity and control over their experiences from the moment they are documented to whatever action they may choose next.

## Section 4

LuLu's competitive advantage lies in its integrated approach to discreet personal safety, private documentation and user-controlled disclosure.




Its differentiation combines an ordinary, fully functional application with concealed access to sensitive records, intelligent contextual and narrative transformation, evidence preservation and continuity across devices.




Rather than requiring users to navigate separate tools for documentation, privacy and support, LuLu brings these capabilities into one coherent experience. Its underlying privacy-oriented technology also creates opportunities for future integration and commercial expansion.

## Section 5

Primary users: Individuals in South Africa who need to privately document experiences involving gender-based violence, domestic abuse, harassment, intimidation, coercion or other personal safety concerns, regardless of gender.




Early adoption communities: University students, young adults and digitally connected communities where awareness, accessibility and trusted referrals can support adoption.




Secondary users: Individuals seeking discreet personal documentation, sensitive journalling and greater control over personal information.




Potential institutional customers and partners: NGOs, victim-support organisations, educational institutions, community programmes and other organisations supporting people affected by abuse or personal safety concerns.
