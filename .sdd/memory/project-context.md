# LuLu — current context

Updated 23 October 2026. Start with [the corrected product vision](product-vision.md), supplied by the owner as the replacement for prior architecture/handoff framing. It defines intended behaviour, not a completed technical specification.

## Product direction

Write naturally; LuLu automatically recognises and coherently conceals people, places, times, relationships and contextual details. Subtle responsive indicators make associations inspectable. In-place corrections are optional, not a mandatory approval workflow. Records remain retrievable through the author's original words and meaning. Aliases can change while underlying links persist.

Real associations remain under device-side control, accessible through a passkey-mediated protected experience; exiting ends protected access. The everyday outer app and its activation interaction are open design questions. Storybook/writing, notes and tasks are candidates; none is selected.

## Engineering freedom and target

Work toward an effective, compelling web-first hackathon demo. Investigate and choose methods to realise the intended experience; do not carry over an earlier fixed component list or mandatory per-entry review step. Local/edge transformation is the target. The demo's processing placement, compromises, storage representation and recovery approach are research/design decisions, not settled architecture.

OpenAI Decisions API is the selected demo decision technology, one service among others. Do not prescribe its exact placement as a second-pass classifier from an earlier handoff. Cloud record synchronisation and hosted model calls are separate boundaries. Customer/admin web applications and a future mobile direction are intended; Firebase, Capacitor, TypeScript monorepo and Cloudflare domains remain candidates.

## Lessons retained

- Preserve coherent entity identity separately from display aliases.
- Distinguish authentication, protected sessions, key custody and record confidentiality when designing their implementation.
- Transform contextual references as well as names; evaluate using representative synthetic writing.
- Continued access across enrolled devices and user-led re-association are different recovery experiences worth exploring.
- Reuse Confessions experience where useful; do not assume its historical spec equals current code or its identity mechanism supplies LuLu's entire privacy engine.
- Keep a future standalone KHÔRA privacy business separate from the hackathon deliverable.

These lessons inform engineering work; they are not new user-facing hoops or blanket constraints on discovery. Retain accurate implementation claims and SDD traceability while leading with the intended outcome.
