# LuLu

Team KHÔRA’s FNB AOTY Hackathon 2026 entry for safer communities and GBV response.

LuLu turns natural writing into a coherent concealed narrative, recognising people, places, times and relationships with subtle visual feedback and optional corrections. Its author can retrieve the record through their original words and meaning, with private associations available through protected access.

Read [current product vision](product-vision.md), then [project context](project-context.md) and [current state](current-state/active-context.md) for subsequent decisions. The owner selected a mobile-first journal/storybook. Its wider safety purpose includes documentation, evidence continuity, selective disclosure and support. The engineering route remains under discussion.

SDD is installed; Phase 001 requirements are a draft, and an independently authorised local prototype exists. Frontend design acceptance and implementation-phase approval remain pending.
