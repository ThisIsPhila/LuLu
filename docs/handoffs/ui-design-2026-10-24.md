## Owner corrections to carry forward

- Categories/entry groupings need compact, meaningful stacks that open on interaction, leaving useful negative space in the hero. The current two large accordion covers are rejected. Determine the right visual and interaction pattern from the actual references before implementation; do not assume a carousel, auto-rotating testimonials or generic accordion is equivalent to the requested journal stack.
- Writing belongs next to the mobile keyboard, with recognition/transformation above it. Preserve this ordering and verify on narrow screens with the keyboard visible where possible.
- Keep headers clear of the camera area and respect safe-area insets. Do not centre important controls in that area.
- Remove exposed Protected categories, vault-status/enclave labels and distinctive sensitive-entry badges from ordinary home. The existing shield/vault entrance may remain temporarily; its final entrance is unresolved.
- Preserve useful controls, logo, content and unrelated interactions during corrections. Do not interpret a targeted correction as permission to scrap the rest of the design.
- Give the owner full visible URLs when sharing previews.

## Bounded research starting points

These are candidates, not selections or endorsements for this product:

- Swiper cards effect: https://swiperjs.com/demos#effect-cards ; React integration: https://swiperjs.com/react . Inspect touch interaction, card layering, keyboard navigation, bundle impact and whether the interaction fits opening a journal collection.
- Aceternity Card Stack: https://ui.aceternity.com/components/card-stack . Its published default is timed testimonial cycling; that default is not the owner's requested interaction. Assess reuse of its visual layering only if useful, and verify licensing/dependencies before adopting code.
- Motion drag primitives: https://motion.dev/docs/react-drag . Lower-level option if an existing ready-made component does not meet the reference; avoid custom gesture work when a suitable maintained component is available. Do not buy Motion+ or any paid asset without owner direction.

Keep research short and evidence-based. Compare a small shortlist against the real use case, available dependencies, touch/keyboard accessibility, reduced motion and implementation effort. Prefer the simplest existing fit. If references leave a material interaction ambiguous, ask the owner a single concrete question with examples rather than claiming certainty. Then implement the selected direction modularly and show visual evidence. No new infrastructure, Firebase project recreation or deployment is authorised by this UI handoff.
