1. [Brand world](01-brand-world.png) — identity mood and tagline.
2. [Identity system](02-identity-system.png) — wordmark applications and spacing concepts.
3. [Colour and type](03-colour-and-type.png) — proposed palette and typography pairing.
4. [Imagery and materials](04-imagery-and-materials.png) — photography, light and surface direction.
5. [Brand in use](05-brand-in-use.png) — concept print and campaign mockups, explicitly not app UI.

## Transparent logo files

These transparent PNG cutouts live in `public/media/brand/`:

- [LuLu wordmark, black](../../../../public/media/brand/lulu-wordmark-black.png)
- [LuLu wordmark, white](../../../../public/media/brand/lulu-wordmark-white.png)
- [Lu short mark, black](../../../../public/media/brand/lu-mark-black.png)
- [Lu short mark, white](../../../../public/media/brand/lu-mark-white.png)

These are direct cutouts from the custom wordmark artwork in [preferred Logo Direction 02](../../references/03-logo-direction-02.png), not font-based redraws. The full wordmark exports are 540 × 215 px; the “Lu” short-mark exports are 270 × 215 px. Both colorways have alpha transparency and can be used over other surfaces. Use black on light surfaces and white on dark surfaces.

## Rejected icon studies

The folded-page, open-journal, layered-story and unfolding-line studies were rejected and moved out of the public app assets. They are preserved under [rejected app icon concepts](../rejected-app-icon-concepts/) for internal history only.

Use the standalone transparent logo exports above in the product. The five sheets define the current brand baseline; their campaign imagery is illustrative and does not specify product functionality or a responsive page layout. Product page designs are tracked separately in `docs/design/stitch_lulu_mobile_journal_experience/`.
