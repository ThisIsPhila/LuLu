Fantastic and now for the last set, let's get the planning
The final batch covers screens 11–14, completing LuLu's onboarding journey.

The first ten screens have introduced everyday writing, personalisation, private access, paragraph protection and quick exit. The final four should demonstrate what happens after an experience has been captured: preserving it, understanding it, finding support and securing the user's space.

The progression is:

Capture → Understand → Connect → Secure.

Screen 11: Some moments need more than words.
11/14 · Multimodal capture and evidence

Purpose: Demonstrate LuLu's ability to capture experiences through different formats and preserve them as records.

Headline: Some moments need more than words.

Supporting copy: “Write it, record it, photograph it or keep the files that matter.”

Visual composition

A clean, functional capture interface featuring four distinct actions:

Text

Voice

Photo

Files

Underneath, a compact example of a saved evidence entry: a recording, photograph and short written note grouped under one record with a timestamp.

A subtle caption, “Originals preserved. Additions stay traceable.”, introduces the legal documentation capability without requiring a technical explanation.

Interactive behaviour: Selecting Voice, Camera or Files introduces the relevant OS permission at the point of use, preceded by a short explanation. In a guided demonstration, those actions can use sample media so users can continue without granting access.

Design: Light, minimal, interface-focused. Distinct capture controls and one evidence preview are sufficient. No cinematic photograph needed.

Screen 12: Understand what you're experiencing.
12/14 · Privacy-aware interpretation

Purpose: Demonstrate LuLu's decision layer and how it can help someone understand possible patterns without making diagnoses.

Headline: A little clarity can change everything.

Supporting copy: “Explore possible patterns, understand your options and decide what happens next.”

Visual composition

The screen demonstrates a transformation from a selected private entry into a structured interpretation.

EXAMPLE · PRIVATE ENTRY

“I keep having to ask permission before I can use my own money.”

Possible topic

Financial control
Some forms of controlling behaviour involve restricting access to money or financial decisions.

Learn about this topic

The example shows that LuLu identifies a potential topic, explains why it may be relevant and makes further information available.

A small disclosure indicator can show that identifying details are checked before external processing.

Design: A slightly more structured screen, but with one central interpretation card rather than a dashboard full of statistics. The result should feel explanatory, measured and respectful.

Screen 13: You don't have to do this alone.
13/14 · Companions and professional support

Purpose: Introduce the user's options for seeking help while remaining in control of their information.

Headline: You don't have to do this alone.

Supporting copy: “Share with someone you trust, explore available help or take your time.”

Visual composition

One central support panel with three clearly differentiated pathways:

Trusted companion

Share selected records, receive contributions or start a safety Trail.

Professional support

Find counselling, legal assistance and verified support services.

Keep it private

Continue documenting without sharing anything.

This screen demonstrates the decision to involve others, rather than asking the user to add a trusted companion immediately.

Design: Warm, calm, approachable. The three pathways should feel equally legitimate, without visual pressure towards disclosure.

Screen 14: Your space. Your control.
14/14 · Security setup and completion

Purpose: Convert the guided experience into a protected, usable personal workspace.

Headline: Your space. Your control.

Supporting copy: “Protect your private space with a passkey. What you choose to share stays your decision.”

Visual composition

A spacious final screen with a clear security-setup component.

Protect private access

Use your device's passkey authentication to unlock LuLu's private space.

Private originals are stored on your device by default.

External processing and sharing follow your privacy permissions.

Interactive behaviour: This is the actual passkey-enrolment stage rather than a simulation. Successful setup transitions to the ordinary LuLu workspace.

Design: Minimal and confident, with the security action as the clear focal point. A restrained transition and haptic acknowledgement can confirm completion.

For the image generation, we should preserve the visual continuity of the previous batches: four proportional portrait screens on one 16:9 canvas, a quiet neutral surrounding background, consistent LuLu branding, strong typographic hierarchy, subtle elevation and appropriate negative space.

All four remain onboarding screens, with only previous/continue controls and progress labelled 11/14 through 14/14. No application tab bars or device mockups.

The final screen should feel like a natural culmination of what the user has just experienced, rather than a promotional closing page.
