## Direction retained

- Warm ivory, paper, sand and stone with restrained sage, soft material depth, organic imagery and editorial type; avoid flat white layouts.
- Use the actual black logo on light surfaces and the actual white logo on a sufficiently contrasting dark, image, sage or stone treatment. Do not redraw the wordmark.
- Shared header has logo, search and avatar/account actions. No header plus. Put Write within lower reach.
- Ordinary navigation and categories stay discreet. Do not expose private/protected status or previews on ordinary screens.
- Sheets suit contextual filters, history/recovery choices, representations and confirmations; full screens suit durable destinations and focused multi-step tasks.

The six [warm/tactile references](resources/2026-10-24-warm-tactile/) remain the broader visual source. The component board and earlier page studies are references, not implementation evidence or a functional specification.
